About us

Built to run townships as one connected system

Integrated Town Management Corporation (ITMC) was formed to give master-planned communities a single operating layer — one platform coordinating security, traffic, facilities, and resident services across every township we manage.

Our mandate

Operate shared township infrastructure so each community stays safe, efficient, and pleasant to live in.

Our approach

Pair on-the-ground teams with software that automates the repetitive parts of township operations.

Our footprint

12+ townships nationwide, from urban business districts to island tourism estates.

Our promise

Faster response times, clearer reporting, and fewer manual handoffs for every stakeholder.
